Visual Routines

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 17, 2026

🔒 Visual Routines is designed with privacy first. All your data stays on your device — we don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Data Storage

Visual Routines stores all data locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This includes:

  • Routines you create
  • Activity progress and completion status
  • App settings and preferences
  • Custom activities you define

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Device information
  • Location data
  • Any other form of user data

No Account Required

Visual Routines does not require user registration or sign-in. There are no accounts, no passwords, and no cloud synchronization.

No Internet Required

After the initial download, Visual Routines works completely offline. No internet connection is needed to use the app.

Third-Party Services

Visual Routines does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Visual Routines is designed to be used by children under parental supervision. Since we do not collect any data, there is no children's data to protect. The app is safe for use by children of all ages.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can:

  • Delete individual routines within the app
  • Clear all app data from the Settings page
  • Uninstall the app to remove all associated data

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
