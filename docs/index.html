Why Visual Routines Matter

Research shows that visual schedules help children develop independence, reduce anxiety, and build executive functioning skills.

  • ✓ Reduces morning chaos and power struggles
  • ✓ Builds independence and self-confidence
  • ✓ Especially helpful for ADHD and autism
  • ✓ Creates predictable, calming structure
